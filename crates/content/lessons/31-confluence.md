[[steps]]
heading = "What confluence is"
text = "**Confluence** means **two or more independent reasons** pointing at the same zone. One reason is a place to watch. Several reasons at the same place mean more traders are likely watching it, so a reaction there is more likely."
why = "Lesson 24 already gave you one example: a round number that's also an old turning point is stronger than either alone."

[[steps]]
heading = "The three this course uses"
text = "Look for: a **Fibonacci level**, ideally 50–61.8% (Lesson 30); an **old support or resistance zone**, often the high the leg broke, now flipped (Lesson 24); and a **round number**, a $50 or $100 level. Two of the three is good; all three is rare and worth noting."
why = "CME Group's lessons show markets pausing at old highs and lows and broken resistance turning into support, and its Fibonacci course treats the 38.2–61.8% levels as places to watch for the trend to resume."

[[steps]]
heading = "The Classic + Fib setup"
text = "Put it together: **H4 trend** → draw the **impulse leg** → wait for the **pullback** into a confluence zone on H1 → a **confirmation candle closes** there → **stop** beyond its wick and the zone → **target** the next zone or an extension, at least **twice** the risk → size for about 1%."
why = "It's the pullback play from Lesson 27 with a sharper zone. Stage 8 writes it into your Trading Plan as this course's main setup."

[[steps]]
heading = "Never against the trend"
text = "Confluence doesn't override the **higher-timeframe trend**. A support zone with three reasons inside an H4 **downtrend** is still a buy against the trend: skip it. The setup only exists in the direction H4 is already going."
why = "Against the trend, even a strong zone tends to break: the bigger move is pushing through it. Lesson 22's rule still holds: the higher timeframe wins."

[[steps]]
heading = "More lines isn't more truth"
text = "Don't keep adding indicators until something lines up; you'll always find a match. Each reason should come from something **different**: a ratio, past price action, a round number. And however many reasons meet, the **stop** stays."
why = "Two Fibonacci grids drawn on different legs aren't two reasons; they're the same idea twice."
