[[steps]]
heading = "Gold wears a dollar price tag"
text = "XAUUSD is gold priced in US dollars. If the dollar gets stronger, each dollar buys more, so it usually takes **fewer dollars** to buy an ounce of gold: the price tends to fall. If the dollar weakens, it usually takes **more dollars**, and the price tends to rise."
why = "Think of buyers outside the US. When the dollar strengthens, gold costs them more in their own currency, so some buy less. Less demand at the same price tends to push the dollar price down."

[[steps]]
heading = "Measuring the dollar: the DXY"
text = "Traders watch the **US Dollar Index (DXY)**: the dollar measured against a fixed basket of six major currencies. When DXY rises, the dollar is getting stronger against them; when it falls, weaker. You'll note its direction each morning in your Pre-Session Checklist."
why = "The basket is weighted heavily towards Europe: the old European currencies, now the euro, make up about 58% of it. The others are the yen, the pound, the Canadian dollar, the Swedish krona and the Swiss franc. So DXY often tells you mostly what the euro did."

[[steps]]
heading = "A seesaw, most of the time"
text = "Because of the price tag, gold and the dollar **often move in opposite directions**. The World Gold Council counts a weaker dollar among the things that support gold, and a stronger dollar among the things that weigh on it."
why = "The WGC groups gold's drivers into economic growth, risk and uncertainty, opportunity cost (the dollar and interest rates, Lesson 17) and momentum. The dollar is one driver among several, which is why it doesn't explain every move."

[[steps]]
heading = "When the seesaw breaks"
text = "In a scare, investors can buy **both** dollars and gold as safe places for money, so both rise together. Other times gold moves on its own news while the dollar sits still. Expect the link to break for days or weeks."
why = "That's why this course treats the dollar as context, not a signal. A rising dollar is a headwind for a gold buy, not a reason to sell. Your trades still come from your setup at a level, with a stop."
