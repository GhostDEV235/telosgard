[[steps]]
heading = "Two different questions"
text = "Every closed trade answers two questions: **did I follow the plan?** and **did it make money?** A **losing trade** said yes, then no: it's simply the cost of the strategy. A **bad trade** broke a rule, and stays bad even when it wins."
why = "This is what Plan Adherence measures in your journal: whether each trade followed the plan, not whether it made money. Live Readiness asks for 90% adherence and doesn't require profit."

[[steps]]
heading = "FOMO: chasing the move"
text = "**FOMO** (fear of missing out) is jumping into a move that's already running: no zone, no confirmation, the stop far away or missing. The fix is the pullback play (Lesson 27): if you missed it, **wait for the next setup**. There always is one."
why = "Chasing means entering after the move, where the honest stop is far away, so the size must be tiny or the reward-to-risk falls apart."

[[steps]]
heading = "Revenge and the moving stop"
text = "After a loss, the urge is to **win it back now**: bigger size, a weaker setup. Close cousin: **moving the stop further away** as price nears it, turning a planned −1R into −3R. Both swap your plan for a feeling. The Daily Loss Limit (Lesson 35) is the brake."
why = "CME Group's lesson on taking losses explains the pull: people hate losses more than they like risk, so traders cut winners early and hold losers too long. A Federal Reserve Bank of New York paper notes that dealers use stop-loss orders as a self-discipline device for the same reason."

[[steps]]
heading = "Overtrading after wins"
text = "A winning streak feels like skill and invites **overtrading**: more trades, looser setups, bigger size. Your plan's maximum trades per day and fixed 1% risk don't change because the week went well. Confidence is not a setup."
why = "After a run of wins it's easy to start counting the setups you want rather than the ones on your checklist. Same rules on good days and bad."

[[steps]]
heading = "Grade the process"
text = "After each trade, ask: was it in my **checklist's Scenarios**, at a zone, with a confirmation candle, a stop beyond structure, a size from the calculator, inside my limits? Yes to all, and a loss is still a **good trade**. That's the habit Stage 8 builds into your journal."
why = "Over 50 Demo Trades, process is the part you control. A plan with positive expectancy (Lesson 34) does the rest, given enough trades."
