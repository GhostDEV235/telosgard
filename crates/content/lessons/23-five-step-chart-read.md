[[steps]]
heading = "Step 1: choose your timeframes"
text = "Decide which charts you'll use before you look: **H4 for direction, H1 for entries** (Lesson 22). Writing the pair down stops you hopping between charts later to find one that agrees with you."
why = "Swing traders use D1 and H4 instead. Whatever the pair, it stays the same for the whole read."

[[steps]]
heading = "Step 2: mark swing highs and lows"
text = "On the higher timeframe, mark the last few **swing highs** (peaks with lower candles on both sides) and **swing lows** (dips with higher candles on both sides). Mark the obvious turns, not every wiggle. They're the skeleton of the chart."
why = "Some traders define a swing high as a candle with two lower highs on each side. The exact rule matters less than being consistent and marking the turns anyone would notice."

[[steps]]
heading = "Step 3: name the structure"
text = "Read the swings from left to right. Highs and lows both rising: an **uptrend**. Both falling: a **downtrend**. Neither: **sideways**, a range. Lesson 25 adds the precise names; for now, one word is enough."
why = "CME Group lists what charts help you find: whether a market is trending, signs of a reversal, and periods of consolidation (sideways). Step 3 answers the first question and watches for the others."

[[steps]]
heading = "Step 4: levels and the session range"
text = "Draw horizontal zones where price has turned before, usually at those swing points, and mark today's **Asian high and low** (Lesson 14). These are the places where something may happen. Stage 5 shows how to draw zones properly."
why = "Round numbers such as 4,100.00 often act as levels too; Lesson 24 explains why. Two or three zones near the current price are plenty."

[[steps]]
heading = "Step 5: only then, the latest candles"
text = "Now read the last few candles on the lower timeframe (Lesson 21), **in context**: is price near one of your levels? Moving with the trend or against it? A big candle in the middle of nowhere means little."
why = "The same candle can mean very different things: a sellers' candle in an uptrend, far from any level, is usually noise; at a level, it's worth watching."

[[steps]]
heading = "Context, not a signal"
text = "The read tells you where you are, not what to do. It ends with a **plan**: which level you're waiting for and what you'd need to see there. Trades still come only from your setup, with a stop."
why = "The read feeds your Pre-Session Checklist: the higher-timeframe bias, the key zones and the day's Scenarios. Stage 5 gives you the setups to look for at those zones."
