[[steps]]

text = "Treat the area between **50% and 61.8%** as a **zone**, like the zones in Lesson 24. Price rarely turns on the exact cent. When it reaches the zone, wait for a **confirmation candle** in the trend's direction to **close** there, then trade as in the pullback play."
why = "Fidelity's guide describes the levels as possible support or resistance areas: possible, not certain."

[[steps]]
heading = "78.6%: where the play ends"
text = "This course draws one more line at **78.6%**. If a candle **closes beyond it**, price has given back almost the whole leg: the pullback play on that leg is **over**. Your stop usually sits just beyond it, so a failed setup costs a planned loss."
why = "78.6% as the end of the play is this course's rule, not a law of the market. Below 100% (the leg's start), the leg is broken anyway (Lesson 29)."

[[steps]]
heading = "Extensions as targets"
text = "**Extensions** project past the end of the leg. In an uptrend, **127.2%** sits 27.2% of the leg **above the high**, and **161.8%** sits 61.8% above it. Use them as **targets** when no older zone is in the way, and check the reward is still at least twice the risk."
why = "CME Group: Fibonacci ratios can also chart the potential extension of a breakout, giving zones where it may end or pause. Charting tools label them from the leg's start, so 127.2% is 1.272 times the leg away from the low."

[[steps]]
heading = "Why they sometimes work"
text = "There's nothing magic in the ratios. Fibonacci levels work, when they do, because **many traders watch the same levels**, just like round numbers. That's why they matter most when they line up with something else, which is Lesson 31."
why = "If a level only works because others watch it, an unusual grid nobody else draws is worth little. Draw it the standard way (Lesson 29)."
