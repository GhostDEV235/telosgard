[[steps]]
heading = "Let it settle"
text = "Your **No-Trade Window** (5 minutes either side, Lesson 15) is the minimum. For this play, wait for the **first H1 candle after the release to close**. For the **FOMC** at 2 pm, also let the press conference start at 2:30. The first minutes are the splash."
why = "A study of gold's reaction to Fed rate decisions found that price and volatility keep adjusting for longer than five minutes after the announcement."

[[steps]]
heading = "Read the direction"
text = "After the settle, ask the top-down questions (Lesson 22). Did price **hold one way** and **break H1 structure** (Lesson 25)? Does that agree with the **H4 trend**, and with the surprise (Lesson 19: hot inflation usually weighs on gold)? If the move fully reversed, there is no direction: no trade."
why = "A 2001 study of COMEX gold futures found that, among 23 US announcements, the employment report, GDP, CPI and personal income had the greatest impact on gold. Those are the releases worth this play."

[[steps]]
heading = "Join it on a pullback"
text = "Don't chase the spike. Wait for price to **pull back** to a zone, often the level the news move just broke, and for a **confirmation candle** to close in the new direction. It's the Classic pullback play (Lesson 27), run after news."
why = "The pullback gives you a defined place to be wrong, a closer stop and a better reward-to-risk than the bottom or top of a spike."

[[steps]]
heading = "Bigger candles, smaller size"
text = "Check **ATR** again: after news it's often much bigger. Put the stop 1–1.5 × the **current** ATR beyond the confirmation wick. A further stop means a **smaller size** for the same risk. If the next zone gives less than 2:1, skip."
why = "Your risk per trade stays the same in dollars; only the position shrinks. That's what keeps a volatile day from becoming an expensive one."

[[steps]]
heading = "When to skip"
text = "Skip if the spread is still wide, another release is due within the hour, the move has already reached the next H4 zone, or the reaction fights the H4 trend. Add the play to a **new plan version** before using it with real money."
why = "News days produce plenty of movement and few clean setups. Most of this play is waiting."
