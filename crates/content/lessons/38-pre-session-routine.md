[[steps]]
heading = "Why a routine"
text = "The **Pre-Session Checklist** turns your thinking into a few minutes before the session, while you're calm. It's a Hard Rule: on any day you trade, a trade without a saved checklist **breaks your plan**. Find it in the **Journal** tab: Start checklist."
why = "Doing the thinking before the session means that during it you're only checking whether a plan you already wrote is happening, which is far easier than deciding from scratch with price moving."

[[steps]]
heading = "1 · The day's news"
text = "List today's **high-impact US releases** and their times: CPI, jobs, FOMC (Lesson 18). The checklist links the official BLS and Fed calendars. Each one opens a **No-Trade Window** of 5 minutes either side (Lesson 15). No big news? Leave it empty."
why = "The Bureau of Labor Statistics publishes its release schedule (CPI, the jobs report) and the Federal Reserve publishes FOMC meeting dates well in advance, so there's no excuse for being surprised."

[[steps]]
heading = "2 · Overnight and mood"
text = "Note the **Asian session's high and low**: London often tests them (Lesson 14). Then one line on the **macro mood**: is the dollar stronger or weaker, are yields up or down, is the market risk-on or risk-off (Lessons 16–20)?"
why = "The mood line isn't a forecast. It's context, so a sudden move doesn't confuse you: a weaker dollar and falling yields are the backdrop where gold tends to find buyers."

[[steps]]
heading = "3 · Bias, zones, Scenarios"
text = "Write the **H4 bias** (up, down or range), **3–5 key zones** as two prices each, and **1–3 Scenarios**: \"If price does X at level Y, I do Z.\" Only trades that match a Scenario follow the plan; your journal asks which one each trade was."
why = "CME Group's trade-plan course suggests citing specific examples of trades you will or will not make. A Scenario is exactly that, written for today."

[[steps]]
heading = "4 · Risk for today"
text = "Commit to the day's **risk per trade** (1% or less), the **Daily Loss Limit** in R (2R), and your balance; add the stop distances you expect, to see their sizes ahead of time. Then **save**. The checklist shows as done for today."
why = "Deciding the risk numbers before the session is what makes them limits rather than suggestions (Lesson 35)."
