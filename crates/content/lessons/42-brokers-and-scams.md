[[steps]]
heading = "You trade against the dealer"
text = "Trading gold CFDs or spot forex means trading **off-exchange**: when you buy, your **dealer** sells. It controls the platform and the prices you see, and if it goes bust your deposit **may not be protected**. That's why who your broker is matters so much."
why = "The CFTC's customer advisory spells this out: you trade only against your dealer, the dealer controls the platform and the prices on it, and your deposits are not protected if the dealer disappears or goes bankrupt."

[[steps]]
heading = "Check the licence yourself"
text = "Look the firm up on the **regulator's own register**: the FCA's Firm Checker in the UK, **NFA BASIC** in the US, ASIC's registers in Australia (Lesson 12). Use the **website and phone number listed there**, not the ones the firm gave you. No listing, no account."
why = "The FCA says dealing with an unauthorised firm means no access to the Financial Ombudsman Service or the compensation scheme if things go wrong. The CFTC advises checking registration and disciplinary history on NFA BASIC before depositing."

[[steps]]
heading = "The red flags"
text = "Walk away if someone **contacts you first** on social media or a dating app, moves you to **WhatsApp or Telegram**, promises **big or guaranteed returns**, takes **only crypto**, has **no real address**, or offers leverage above legal limits. One flag is enough."
why = "These come straight from the CFTC's list of common warning signs for forex fraud, which notes that many frauds start on social media."

[[steps]]
heading = "Signals and \"account managers\""
text = "Paid **signal groups** sell other people's trades, not a plan you understand. An **\"account manager\"** who wants your login or to trade for you is a red flag on its own. Never share your **password**, and never let anyone trade your account for you."
why = "The CFTC warns that salespeople and influencers may be paid per customer they bring, with no trading expertise, and that those relationships are often hidden from you."

[[steps]]
heading = "Withdrawals tell the truth"
text = "Before funding properly, **read the withdrawal terms** and test a small withdrawal. If you're ever told to pay a fee, a \"tax\" or a bigger deposit **before you can withdraw**, it's a scam. Stop paying and report it to the regulator."
why = "The CFTC: fraudulent dealers commonly refuse withdrawals until customers pay made-up commissions or taxes or invest more; you should never have to pay more money to get your money back."
