# Trade School: Gold (XAUUSD) Course

A self-paced course that takes a complete beginner from zero to trading Gold against the US Dollar, with understanding checked before each new concept.

## Language

**Course**:
The whole ordered path of learning, from first principles to trading-ready.
_Avoid_: program, curriculum (when meaning the product)

**Stage**:
An ordered group of **Lessons** that share a theme (for example Stage 1 Foundations). Shown as one card on the home screen.

**Lesson**:
One concept, taught in plain language with an analogy, a real-life example and optional video references.
_Avoid_: module, chapter, unit (unless grouping is introduced later)

**Lesson Step**:
One idea of a **Lesson** on its own screen: a short heading and a few sentences, with optional deeper detail behind a "Why?" toggle. A Lesson is read step by step.

**Widget**:
An interactive control inside a **Lesson Step** that lets the **Learner** move one number and watch one result change (for example, dragging the sell price to see profit or loss per ounce). Practice, not assessment.

**Spaced Review**:
Two or three **Quiz** questions from earlier **Lessons** shown at the start of a new one, chosen mostly from questions the **Learner** got wrong or answered while **Guessing**. Practice only; it never blocks progress.

**Confidence**:
The **Learner**'s own "Sure" or "Guessing" tag on each **Quiz** answer. A right answer tagged Guessing still counts towards the **Gate**, but is treated as not yet learned for **Spaced Review**, so honesty never costs the **Learner** anything.

**Question Record**:
How one **Quiz** question has gone for the **Learner** over time: times answered, wrong, and right-but-guessing. Feeds **Spaced Review** and shows which questions explain things poorly.

**Recall Set**:
Three quick questions at the end of a **Lesson**'s steps, answered from memory before the **Quiz**. Practice, not part of the **Gate**.

**Quiz**:
Multiple-choice questions at the end of a **Lesson** that check whether the learner *recognises* correct ideas.

**Explain-Back**:
A free-text question where the learner explains a concept in their own words; Claude grades it and corrects misunderstandings. Checks whether the learner *understands*, not just recognises.
_Avoid_: essay, open question

**Chart Drill**:
A practice exercise on an illustrative candle chart (spot a pattern, mark a level, name the structure). It trains the eye; it is separate from the **Gate**.

**Grade**:
Claude's ruling on an **Explain-Back**: **Got it** (understood), **Almost** (a named gap to fix, then rewrite) or **Not yet** (re-taught with a different analogy, then retry). Almost and Not yet also show a **Suggested Answer**: the Learner's own answer with the missing idea added and highlighted, to learn from before rewriting it in their own words.

**Gate**:
The rule that locks the next **Lesson**: at least 80% on the **Quiz** *and* a **Got it** on the **Explain-Back**. Retries are unlimited and there is no penalty. Lessons cannot be skipped. Once passed, a Lesson stays passed.

**Milestone**:
A learning achievement the course marks with a celebration: passing a **Lesson**'s **Gate**, completing a **Stage**, or reaching **Live Readiness**. A profitable trade is never a Milestone; the course rewards discipline, not profit.
_Avoid_: achievement, reward, streak

**Dollar Move**:
The course's unit for how far gold's price travels: the change in USD per ounce (2650.00 → 2655.00 is a $5 move). Stops, targets, journal fields and the **Trading Plan** are always in dollar moves.

**Point**:
$0.01 of gold price, the smallest price step on most MT5 brokers.

**Pip**:
$0.10 of gold price (10 **Points**). Taught as a defined term but never used as the course's working unit, because outside sources use "pip" to mean $0.01, $0.10 or $1.00.
_Avoid_: using "pips" without converting to a **Dollar Move**

**Lot**:
Trade size. 1 standard lot = 100 ounces, so a $1 **Dollar Move** = $100 profit or loss per lot (0.01 lot = $1).

**Price Action**:
Trading from the price chart itself (candles, levels and structure) rather than from indicators. Within this course it means **Classic Price Action** unless stated otherwise.

**Classic Price Action**:
The **Learner**'s first strategy: read the trend from market structure on a higher timeframe, mark key support and resistance, and enter only on a confirmation candle (pin bar or engulfing candle) at a level, in the trend's direction. It covers two plays: a **pullback** to a level within the trend, and a **bounce** off a level tested at least twice.
_Avoid_: trend pullback strategy, S/R bounce strategy (both are Classic Price Action)

**Fibonacci Retracement**:
A grid drawn over one completed impulse move, marking where a pullback often pauses (38.2%, 50%, 61.8%). A reference, never a signal on its own.

**Confluence**:
Two or more independent reasons pointing at the same price, such as a key level that is also a Fibonacci level or a round number. Stronger than any one reason alone.

**ATR**:
Average True Range: how far gold normally moves in one candle on a given timeframe. Used to check a stop sits beyond normal noise (at least 1–1.5× ATR beyond structure).

**Supply & Demand** / **Smart Money Concepts (SMC)**:
Later price-action methods layered on top of **Classic Price Action**, never taught as replacements for it.

**Breakout & Retest** / **Asian-Range Breakout** / **News Follow-Through**:
Later strategies, also layered on **Classic Price Action**: trading the retest of a broken level; trading the break of the Asian session's high or low as London opens; trading the direction gold settles into after a major news release (never the release candle itself).

**Core Track**:
The **Lessons** that take the **Learner** from zero to placing **Demo Trades** under a written **Trading Plan**. Completing it is the course's main finish line.

**Live Track**:
The final **Lessons** on moving to real money (psychology, journaling discipline, broker choice). Locked until the **Learner** has earned **Live Readiness**.

**Advanced Track**:
The **Lessons** on further strategies, locked until **Live Readiness**: **Breakout & Retest**, **Asian-Range Breakout**, **News Follow-Through**, **Supply & Demand** and **SMC**. Adopting one means writing a new version of the **Trading Plan**.

**Demo Trade**:
A practice trade placed with a broker's simulated money on real market prices.
_Avoid_: paper trade, practice trade (use one term)

**Trading Plan**:
The **Learner**'s written rules for when to enter, where to exit and how much to risk on each trade.
_Avoid_: strategy (a strategy is only one part of the plan)
Its rules are either **Hard Rules** or **Soft Rules**.

**Hard Rule**:
A numeric **Trading Plan** rule that can be checked by calculation alone (max risk % per trade, min risk-to-reward, allowed sessions, max trades per day, stop-loss required, **Daily Loss Limit**, **No-Trade Windows**, a **Pre-Session Checklist** saved for that day).

**Daily Loss Limit**:
A **Hard Rule**: the most the **Learner** may lose in one day, in R (multiples of their risk per trade). Once the day's closed losses reach it, any further entry that day breaks the rule.

**No-Trade Windows**:
A **Hard Rule**: no entries during the daily rollover (about 5–6 pm New York), the Sunday open, or within 5 minutes of a tier-1 release (CPI, NFP, FOMC).

**Soft Rule**:
A **Trading Plan** rule that needs judgement (for example "entered on a confirmation candle at a key level, with the higher-timeframe trend"). Every plan includes one: the trade was one of that day's written **Scenarios**. Judged by Claude from the **Journal Entry**.
A plan is versioned: each **Journal Entry** is judged against the version in force when its trade was taken.

**Live Readiness**:
The state reached by logging at least 50 **Journal Entries** across at least 8 weeks, with at least 90% showing **Plan Adherence**, and without the demo account breaching the **Trading Plan**'s maximum total loss. Profit is not required. It unlocks the **Live Track** and the **Advanced Track**.
A max-loss breach starts a new **Readiness Run**.

**Readiness Run**:
The current attempt at **Live Readiness**, starting from a fresh demo balance. The max total loss is measured against that starting balance. Only **Journal Entries** in the current run count. A max-loss breach ends the run, and the next entry starts a new one.

**Pre-Session Checklist**:
The **Learner**'s dated routine before trading: today's high-impact USD events, the Asian high and low, a one-line macro mood, the higher-timeframe bias, key zones, the day's **Scenarios**, and the risk committed for the day. A **Demo Trade** on a day without one is not adherent.
_Avoid_: daily plan, prep (use one term)

**Scenario**:
A written "if price does X at level Y, I do Z" line in a **Pre-Session Checklist**. Only trades matching a Scenario follow the plan.

**R-Multiple**:
A closed trade's result divided by the money it risked at the stop: +2R won twice the risk, −1R lost exactly the planned amount.
_Avoid_: "risk-reward" for a result (that term is for the plan, before the trade)

**Expectancy**:
The average result per trade in R: (win rate × average win) − (loss rate × average loss). Positive expectancy can come from being right only half the time.

**Emotion Tag**:
The **Learner**'s one-word label for how they felt about a **Demo Trade** (calm, FOMO, revenge, fear, overconfident), set after the trade.

**Weekly Review**:
The **Learner**'s weekly look back at their **Journal Entries**: win rate, average R, and rules broken.

**Journal Entry**:
The **Learner**'s record of one **Demo Trade**: what they did, why they did it, and how it turned out. It can be deleted for 24 hours after it's logged (for test or mistaken entries); after that it is part of the record. Deleting the only entry in the current **Readiness Run** removes that run too.
_Avoid_: log, trade record

**Plan Adherence**:
Whether a **Demo Trade** followed every rule in the **Trading Plan** in force when it was taken. It is judged per trade and is either yes or no. A profitable trade that broke a rule is not adherent.

**Adherence Verdict**:
Claude's yes/no ruling on a **Journal Entry**'s **Plan Adherence**, with the specific rule broken (if any). The **Learner** does not mark their own adherence.

**Dispute**:
The **Learner**'s one-time written challenge to an **Adherence Verdict**. Claude reconsiders it, and the second verdict is final.

**Learner**:
The person taking the **Course**.
_Avoid_: user, student
