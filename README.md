# Telosgard

An interactive Gold (XAUUSD) trading course for a complete beginner. It's built in Rust (Leptos → WebAssembly) with Tailwind and published as a private claude.ai Artifact.

**Open it:** https://claude.ai/artifact/EoVVZJSbw2fdtmXxiyP8WU (private to the owner)

## How to use Telosgard (2 minutes)

1. **Today** is home. The big card is your next lesson. Each stage card shows your progress and a *suggested* pace; it's never a deadline.
2. **A lesson** takes about 2–5 minutes, one step at a time: a question to start, short steps (some with a widget to try), an analogy, a real example, a common mistake, key takeaways, optional videos (they open YouTube) and three Recall questions. Stuck on a step? Ask Claude about it.
3. **Pass the Gate** to open the next lesson: score **80%+ on the quiz** (questions reshuffle, retry as often as you like) **and** get **Got it** on the **Explain-Back**, where you explain the idea in your own words and Claude grades it. *Almost* tells you what's missing; *Not yet* re-explains it another way. Nothing ever passes without a real grade.
4. **Practice** has six quick drills on illustrative prices: calling the next candle, candles, spread, Dollar Moves, pips and order types. They train your eye and don't count towards anything.
5. **Tools** has the position-size calculator and your **Trading Plan** (you write it properly at Lesson 38; every change is saved as a new version).
6. **Journal** is for demo trading (Stage 8 onwards): do the **Pre-Session Checklist** first, then **log each trade**. The app checks the Hard Rules instantly and Claude judges the rest; you can dispute a verdict once. Close the trade with its result, review your week, and watch **Live Readiness**: 50 trades that follow your plan over 8 weeks unlock the Live and Advanced tracks.
7. **Your progress saves to your claude.ai account** (the chip under "Today" says so). The ◐ button switches dark/light.

> The first time it asks, allow Claude (for grading) and storage (for saving). If saving isn't available, the chip says so and every lesson still works.
