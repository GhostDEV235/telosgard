# French edition: terms and style

Telosgard's French edition speaks to the Learner with **tu**: warm, short sentences, "a friend who trades". This file fixes the French name of every course term so lessons, screens and Professor Aurel agree. Market jargon French traders use in English (spread, stop-loss, lot, swap, trading) stays in English, defined on first use.

## Typography

- Typographic apostrophe ( ’ ), French quotes « … » with non-breaking spaces inside.
- A narrow non-breaking space before ? ! ; : (applied by `scripts/fr-typography.py`).
- Amounts: `80 $`, `1 000 $`; decimals with a comma: `0,20 lot`, `4 100,00`. The platform (MetaTrader) may still show `4100.00`; Lesson 4 says so.
- Times in 24-hour style: `8 h 30`, `17 h`, "heure de New York".
- The YouTube videos stay in English, labelled « en anglais ».
