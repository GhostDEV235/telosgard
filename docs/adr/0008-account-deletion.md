# Account Deletion erases the person and keeps anonymised numbers

*Decided 2026-11-13.*

The Master Plan asked both for soft-deleted students (so analytics survive) and for a "delete my account" path. A soft delete keeps the person's email and journals, which is not what "delete my account" promises and not what laws such as the GDPR allow. So **Account Deletion** suspends the Learner for a 7-day grace period (undoable), then erases their identity, sessions, journals, plans, checklists, Explain-Back text and screenshots for good. Events and quiz results are re-labelled under a random ID, so totals and pass rates stay correct without pointing to anyone. The audit log keeps "Learner deleted, by whom, when" with before and after values removed.

The Owner can delete from the dashboard (after re-entering the two-factor code), and Learners can delete their own account from their settings. Support can only suspend.
